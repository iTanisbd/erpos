# Repair Ticket Items

## Purpose

Stores every repair item under a repair ticket.

---

## Example

### Ticket #1001

#### Item 1

**Problem**

- Display Issue

**Service**

- Display Replacement

**Technician**

- Karim

**Price**

- BDT 1,500

**Status**

- Pending

---

#### Item 2

**Problem**

- Battery Issue

**Service**

- Battery Replacement

**Technician**

- Rahim

**Price**

- BDT 800

**Status**

- Completed
