# ADR-002

## Title

Service Catalog Architecture

---

## Decision

Repair Problems and Services are separate entities.

Problem:

"What is wrong?"

Service:

"What work will be performed?"

---

## Reason

This architecture allows:

- Multiple solutions for one problem.
- Different pricing.
- Different technician skills.
- Better reporting.
- Better analytics.
- Future expansion.

---

## Status

Accepted
